關於我們
ABOUT US

在新的城市，
一起活出信仰

Living our faith
in a new city

方濟各青年會是一個由香港移居多倫多的天主教青年組成的獨立平信徒信仰團體。 我們以廣東話為主要語言，在加拿大這個新的生活環境中，一起尋找、分享及活出天主教信仰。

St. Francis of Assisi’s Young Adults is an independent lay Catholic faith community formed by young adults who have immigrated from Hong Kong to Toronto. Cantonese is our primary language as we seek to live and share our Catholic faith together.

我們透過團體生活、祈禱、聖言及服務，彼此陪伴，在多倫多的生活中繼續跟隨基督。

Through community life, prayer, scripture and service, we accompany one another in following Christ in Toronto.

我們的會員 Who We Welcome

18歲或以上、願意跟隨基督，在團體中一起成長的天主教徒。

Catholic young adults aged 18 or above who seek to follow Christ and grow together in faith.

使命與路向
OUR MISSION

信德・望德・愛德

Faith · Hope · Charity

我們以信仰生活為中心，在團體內彼此陪伴，並將所領受的愛帶到日常生活。

Our community is rooted in faith, strengthened by hope, and expressed through charity.

01

信德

Faith

重新燃點信仰之火，通過聖言及禮儀，獲取信德的奧蹟。

To rekindle the fire of faith through Scripture and the liturgy, entering more deeply into the mystery of faith.

02

望德

Hope

通過團體信仰生活，在這團體的愛內，活現望德的神效。

Through community life, to experience and embody the living power of Christian hope in love.

03

愛德

Charity

虔誠侍奉萬軍上主，愛上主所愛的人，擁抱愛德的根源。

To serve the Lord of hosts faithfully, love those whom God loves, and embrace the source of charity.

OUR PATRON

聖方濟各・亞西西

St. Francis of Assisi

聖方濟各以簡樸、喜樂、和平及對天主受造界的愛而聞名。他以福音為生活的核心，並以貧窮、服務及與眾人修和的精神跟隨基督。

St. Francis of Assisi is remembered for his simplicity, joy, peace, love for creation, and radical commitment to the Gospel. His life was marked by service, humility and a deep desire to follow Christ.

對我們而言，聖方濟各提醒我們： 信仰不只是思想或知識，更是在日常生活中活出福音。

For us, St. Francis reminds us that faith is not merely something we learn or think about, but something we live through everyday life.

「天主，使我作你和平的工具。」
“Make me a channel of Your peace.”
